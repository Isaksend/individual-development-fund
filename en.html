Mission

Individual Development Fund creates opportunities for everyone to grow — the scientist and the artist, the entrepreneur and the doctor, the schoolgirl and the young leader. Through education, research and a vibrant community we help people unlock their potential and, together, build a sustainable, fair and creative society.

Formats

From an intimate meetup to an international forum, we choose the format that creates the greatest impact for participants.

  1. 01

    Meetups

    Informal gatherings where professionals and communities share experience.

  2. 02

    Lectures

    Public talks by experts, scientists and practitioners.

  3. 03

    Webinars

    Online sessions open to participants from any region.

  4. 04

    Mentorship Programs

    One-on-one guidance from industry leaders.

  5. 05

    Learning Programs

    Courses and intensives with hands-on assignments and certificates.

  6. 06

    Research
